A journey to ancient romance

Overview

Fukui Prefectural Dinosaur Museum is one of the leading dinosaur museums in the world.
The museum is located in Katsuyama, Fukui, which is known as the treasury of dinosaur fossils of Japan.

The spacious museum building without pillars is filled of various dinosaur skeletons, fossils of other animals and plants, diorama with dinosaur life reconstruction models, and so forth.

Visitors can have a close look at spectacular dinosaur animatronics (robots) showing powerful and realistic motions.
