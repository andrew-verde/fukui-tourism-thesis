Access & Fee

Opening Hours

9:00 - 17:00 (No entry after 16:30)
8:30 - 18:00 (No entry after 17:30)(during the summer holidays)

Closing Day

2nd and 4th Wednesday(except during the summer holidays)
Year-end and New Year holidays

Notice

  • Please refrain from eating, drinking, and smoking inside the museum.
  • Flash photography and the use of tripods are strictly prohibited inside the museum.
  • No pet allowed.(except service dog)

Access

by train

  • Get off at JR Fukui Station, then board Echizen Railway Eiheiji Katsuyama Line, bound for Katsuyama (approximately 1 hour.)
  • Get off at Katsuyama Station, you can catch a taxi there (approximately 10 minutes to Museum.)
  • There is also a community bus running from Katsuyama Station to Museum.
  • Travel time from various regions (not including the transfer time.)
    • From Tokyo /
      Hokuriku Shinkansen, or Tokaido Shinkansen, JR Hokuriku Line Express, Echizen Railway, Community bus --- approximately 4 and half hours
    • From Osaka /
      JR Kosei Line Express, Echizen Railway, Community bus --- approximately 3 hours
    • From Nagoya /
      JR Hokuriku Line Express, Echizen Railway, Community bus --- approximately 3 hours

by car

  • From Fukui-kita JCT･IC (Hokuriku Expressway) via Chubu Jukan Expressway to Katsuyama --- approx. 40 min.
  • From Maruoka IC (Hokuriku Expressway) to Katsuyama --- approx. 40 min.
  • From Shirotori JCT･IC (Tokai-Hokuriku Expressway) via Chubu Jukan Expressway through Ono to Katsuyama --- approx. 90 min.
  • Parking for 1300 cars and 10 motor coaches are available.
  • Travel time from various regions
    • From Tokyo /
      On Tomei, Meishin, Hokuriku Expressway --- approximately 7 hours
    • From Osaka /
      On Meishin, Hokuriku Expressways --- approximately 3 and half hours
    • From Nagoya /
      On Meishin, Hokuriku Expressways --- approximately 2 and half hours
